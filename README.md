# Obsidian Physician Templates

A collection of templates I use as a medical student to think clearly, learn efficiently, and improve patient care.

## What’s included
- Basic Terms template (includes frontmatter for properties) 
- [Lecture Notes](Lecture-Notes.md) template
- PICO / evidence-based medicine template
- Pathology template 

## How to use
1. Download the templates
2. Drop them into your Obsidian Templates folder
3. Customize them to fit your workflow

## Why I made this
Medical training is overwhelming. These templates are part of how I structure thinking, not just information.
